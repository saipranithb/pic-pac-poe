# Independent original-frame review — pre-fix candidate

Reviewed all 96 regular iPhone / regular iPhone AX3 candidate originals individually at full resolution via view_image(detail: original), and 32 canonical Android originals. Per-image source hashes and observations are in the adjacent JSON inventory. A contact sheet did not substitute for individual inspection.

**Verdict: not approved pending two product fixes.** 86 per-image passes; 10 affected images flagged for two defects, not ten independent defects.

1. Dark and light AX3 How to Play: step markers 2., 3., and 4. wrap their periods onto a second line. This is visible broken typography, not intended Dynamic Type reflow.
2. Dark and light AX3 thinking/targeting versus placing/settled: the board changes vertical position by approximately 52pt when the instruction title switches between two lines and one. Although input is locked during these stages, this is an ordinary moving-target visual stability defect.

The root is correcting marker intrinsic width and stable instruction geometry, with actual cell-frame regression tests. These originals must remain available as before evidence, and post-fix originals must be inspected before baseline approval.

## Semantic Android parity

The regular iPhone frames preserve the canonical Form Playground 2.0 hierarchy, warm resin surfaces, Fredoka wordmark character, semantic coral X / pistachio O, distinct actor accents, moulded piece depth and shadow, board proportions, tray structure, and native but intentionally styled controls. Handoff is private. Reveal and result cards give actor/symbol identity and clear actions. Dark/light treatments remain coherent. No additional regular-size visual defect was found.

AX3 adopts stacked player status, vertically stacked difficulty/theme choices and tray information, and native scrolling without horizontal truncation. Reveal and result cards fully fit at the reviewed AX3 viewport. Settings labels wrap without overlapping switches. The tutorial marker and AI-stage geometry defects above are exceptions requiring correction. No additional clipping, actor/symbol ambiguity, tray or theme defect was found among the 96 inspected originals.

## Limits

Bottom fixtures explicitly select defaultScrollAnchor(.bottom); they are not evidence of a user scroll gesture or the production initial scroll offset. Expected top-edge cutoff at that position is not permanent clipping. AX3 AI Lab top and bottom screenshots do not together expose every middle opponent row; interactive/automated scroll coverage remains separate. Still fixtures cannot certify frame timing, real keyboard navigation, VoiceOver speech or physical-device behavior. Cross-platform comparison is semantic; deterministic pixel agreement applies only to calibrated same-toolchain iOS captures.
