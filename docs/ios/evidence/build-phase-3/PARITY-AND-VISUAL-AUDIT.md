# Independent Form Playground 2.0 audit

The Build Phase 2 review was treated as a hypothesis. Every one of its 20 accepted 1206 × 2622 PNGs was opened individually at original resolution through the image viewer, including both themes of Home, human placement, Local handoff/reveal, computer targeting/settlement, result, Settings, How to Play and AI Lab. The viewer can scale a tall image for display; the original file was supplied, without cropping. This review did not substitute the Phase 2 contact sheet, hashes or prior conclusions for visual inspection.

The inspected set is exactly the `captures` inventory in [the Phase 2 manifest](../build-phase-2/runs/phase2-complete-20260921/manifest.json). Canonical Android references were also opened directly: both themed Home scene X frames, Classic, computer targeting/settlement, results, Settings, How to Play and AI Lab; dark human placement/reveal and Local handoff/reveal. The governed Android PNGs are intentionally smaller exports and different platform typography. They establish semantic geometry and intent, not a valid pixel-diff baseline for native iOS.

## Intentional corrections and before evidence

- **Winning-line geometry:** Phase 2 used a 6/3-point shadow/focus pair. The canonical contract specifies a 5-point recess line with a 2-point text-colored center. The final board now uses that geometry, and its target/winning corner radius matches the canonical cell contour. Before: [Phase 2 dark result](../build-phase-2/runs/phase2-complete-20260921/captures/ios-result-dark.png) and [light result](../build-phase-2/runs/phase2-complete-20260921/captures/ios-result-light.png). After: the final baseline result and terminal-settlement captures. No other board/piece redesign was made.
- **Restored speech:** Initial stage observation no longer emits transition announcements on restoration. Target/placement speech includes coordinates, and focusing the same computer instruction no longer duplicates the explicit announcement. Modal focus remains available for access to the current screen.
- **Difficulty selection:** Home retains its selected difficulty through supporting-page navigation using native scene storage.
- **Native resources:** A missing launcher icon was an ordinary codebase defect. The native icon is a reproducible render of the existing canonical adaptive-icon vector paths. A theme-aware launch canvas avoids the default white launch flash. See [native resource audit](NATIVE-RESOURCE-AUDIT.md).
- **Native privacy copy:** Settings now says “internet access,” preserving the offline meaning without claiming an Android-style Internet permission exists on iOS.
- **Probability footer legibility:** The expanded native audit flagged the small held-piece caption. The full frame showed neither clipping nor an overlay; the specified color pair is above 6:1. Its native type role increases from 12-point caption to 13-point medium-weight footnote, preserving the exact palette and wording. The intermediate regular-weight light footer also measured above 6.67:1 in actual pixels; the modest weight increase improves the rendered small type rather than changing the canonical colors. [The uncropped before frame](defects/probability-footer-before.png) and [provenance](defects/probability-footer.json) retain the finding. Apple's [contrast guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) sets 4.5:1 for small text; the numerical palette check supplements the rendered audit rather than replacing it.

The exploratory large-text run exposed a first-boot Apple Intelligence banner over gameplay. That image was rejected, not accepted or cropped. The final capture harness prewarms each disposable simulator, checks a unique restoration marker and central image content, and retains complete screenshots including system UI. Only visually inspected intentional results can become baselines. Pixel comparison has no baseline-writing operation.

The final accepted visual inventory, review record, repeated-capture calibration, temporal evidence and remaining device/runtime boundaries are linked from the consolidated completion report. This document records the comparison method and product decisions; it does not turn simulator evidence into physical-device certification.

## Independent AI Lab audit investigation

The native auditor also flagged the three-line AI Lab introduction after scrolling. Independent inspection of the full original frame found the paragraph complete and unobscured. Direct decoding of the original sRGB PNG measured solid text `#DDC5B5` against canvas `#251F1C` at **9.855906830:1**, above 4.5:1. This is actual screenshot evidence, not an assumed palette pass. The light palette computes to 6.285848983:1, but that number is source-only and is not represented as a sampled light screenshot. [The retained measurement and source context](defects/ai-lab-intro-contrast.json), [read-only reproducible sampler](defects/measure-ai-lab-intro-contrast.py), [original native finding](defects/ai-lab-intro-native-finding.txt), and [uncropped diagnostic frame](defects/scroll-safe-area-before.png) preserve the distinction.

The same diagnostic frame shows a real header/status-area overlap. The contrast calculation does not accept that layout: scroll clipping is corrected separately and fresh-process native audits and final captures must verify the corrected result. This investigation is specific to the reported paragraph and is not a blanket accessibility-audit exception.

The [post-correction diagnostic frame](defects/scroll-safe-area-after.png) shows the status area clear after scrolling, with lower AI Lab content reachable. The native auditor then identified the same introduction at y=−1.7 pt, height 64.3 pt, while content clips at 62 pt: only 0.6 pt of its bounding box intersects the viewport and its text ink is offscreen. [Before/after provenance and exact audit bounds](defects/scroll-safe-area.json) preserve this separate finding. Any handling is restricted to this exact offscreen paragraph/contrast issue after its visible top viewport passes; it cannot waive a visible contrast issue or another screen. Final full-suite evidence remains required.

The later native audit still flagged the fully visible 13-point light probability footer. [Its original full frame and measured-gradient proof](defects/probability-footer-13pt-contrast.json) record 3,065 opaque `#695543` text pixels and all 22 background colors from an adjacent unobscured region on the same scanlines. Actual contrast ranges from **6.676005388:1 to 6.804694696:1**. The vertical card gradient explains the small background variation; antialiased glyph-edge blends are excluded from the authored foreground calculation. This supports preserving the semantic palette while reviewing glyph weight for legibility, and does not exempt visible footer text from the native audit. The reproducible [sampler](defects/measure-footer-13pt-contrast.py) reads original bytes only.
