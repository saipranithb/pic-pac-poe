# Pic-Pac-Poe privacy policy

Effective: 2026-08-26

Pic-Pac-Poe is an offline game. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

- The app requests no Internet permission.
- There are no analytics, advertising, account, Firebase, networking, or cloud-AI SDKs.
- Matches and AI computations run locally on the device.
- Sound, haptics, theme, and reduced-motion preferences are stored locally with Android DataStore.
- Android cloud backup is disabled for the application.

Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored settings according to normal Android behavior.

The open-source project and issue tracker may be hosted by a third-party code platform. Activity on that platform is governed by the platform's own privacy terms and is separate from the Android app.
